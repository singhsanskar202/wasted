# Wasted — launch & marketing plan

Drafted 2026-07-19. The store listing (docs/store-listing.md) is the landing
page; this is everything that drives people to it.

## Positioning — one paragraph, memorise it

**Wasted is the mirror your phone doesn't want you to have.** Every screen
time app either congratulates you (streaks, confetti) or polices you
(blockers you disable within a week). Wasted does neither: it puts the one
number you avoid on your lock screen and keeps it there until you stop
avoiding it. The enemy in all copy is not "screen time" — it's **comfortable
ignorance**. The promise is never "we'll fix you"; it's **"you will know,
everywhere, all day — and knowing is the thing you can't undo."**

Tagline bank (pick one, use everywhere):
- *the number you avoid. kept.*
- *you can't unsee it.*
- *is this how you want to spend your one life?*

## The hook that carries everything: THE GUESS

The app's own reality check is the entire marketing engine. Every piece of
content is some form of: **"guess before you look. now look."** Almost nobody
guesses high, and the gap is the shareable, commentable, life-changing
moment. It works in a tweet, a reel, a Product Hunt tagline, and a
conversation at dinner. Never lead with features; lead with the gap.

## Content that markets itself (build order matters)

1. **The receipt is the growth loop — build share cards before launch.**
   A receipt screenshot ("instagram — 2h 12m … total 4h 05m — 25% of your
   waking hours") is inherently postable confession content. One tap →
   receipt as a clean image with a small `wasted` mark. This was deliberately
   deferred earlier; it is now the single highest-leverage pre-launch build.
   People will not share dashboards; they will absolutely share a bill.
2. **30-second screen recordings, no talking head, no music drops:**
   - The island climbing while reels scroll — cut to the receipt at night.
   - The "you said: play the ukulele." nudge landing mid-scroll. This one is
     the emotional peak of the whole product — lead with it.
   - The habit bell arriving at 8:59pm. Caption: "my phone warns me about
     me now."
3. **The wrapped moment, later**: month-end long-receipt screenshots ("july:
   41h") will do the same job seasonally.

## Channels, in order of expected yield

1. **Reddit** — r/nosurf, r/digitalminimalism, r/getdisciplined, r/ADHD
   (massive overlap with doom-scrolling pain). Post as a builder's story:
   "I guessed 2h. It was 4h40m. So I built a mirror." Honest first-person
   post + screenshots; never marketing-speak. These communities convert AND
   supply App Store review language.
2. **X / Threads — build in public.** You have receipts of your own usage
   falling; that's the story arc people follow. Post your own numbers weekly.
3. **Short video** (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) with the recordings above. The
   product is dark, austere and instantly legible on camera — it reads as an
   aesthetic, which is what travels.
4. **Product Hunt** — after the App Store listing is live and share cards
   exist. Tagline: "Guess how much you scrolled today. Now look."
5. **Press later, and niche**: digital-wellness newsletters and "apps that
   respect you" lists (the no-server privacy story is genuinely rare and is
   the angle journalists can verify themselves).

## What we never say (brand law, applies to ads too)

- No fabricated statistics ("users reduce usage by 40%!") — the mirror
  doesn't lie, including about itself.
- No cure language: "detox", "addiction cured", "get your life back
  guaranteed". The honest claim is confrontation, not salvation.
- No shame-marketing at OTHER people ("your friends are wasting their
  lives") — the mirror only ever faces its owner.
- Never call the notifications "ragebait" publicly. In-house word only.

## Pricing page psychology (already built, stated here for ads)

Free forever daily mirror is the acquisition engine — say it loudly; it
disarms the "another subscription" reflex. Pro is sold with one sentence:
**"the mirror remembers, and arrives early."** (long receipt + habit bell).

## Launch-week sequence

1. Entitlement granted → ship to App Store (paywall ON, products live,
   share cards built).
2. Day 0: Reddit story posts (2 subreddits max, native tone) + X thread.
3. Day 1–3: shorts, one per day (island / you-said / habit bell).
4. Day 4: Product Hunt with the guess hook.
5. Ongoing: weekly own-numbers post; reply to every App Store review
   (the review-response voice: same register as the app, never defensive).

## Success metrics (keep it honest with ourselves)

- Store page conversion (App Store Connect analytics — it's free).
- Guess-screen completion → day-7 retention (the reality check IS the
  activation moment; if D7 is weak, the mirror isn't landing, and no
  marketing fixes that).
- Share-card exports per DAU once built — the only viral metric that matters.
