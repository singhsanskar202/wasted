# Wasted — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [set at publication]**

Wasted is built so that we cannot see your data. This policy is short
because there is almost nothing to disclose.

## The one-sentence version

Your screen-time data is processed entirely on your device, is never
transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we operate no servers that could
receive it.

## What the app accesses

**Screen Time (Apple's FamilyControls / DeviceActivity frameworks).**
With your explicit permission, iOS reports usage thresholds for the apps
*you* select. Apple's framework is designed so that apps like Wasted see
opaque tokens and aggregate durations — Wasted counts your time; it cannot
read your content, messages, or browsing.

## Where your data lives

All usage records, settings, and history are stored in the app's private
container on your device (and in the app's shared container used by its own
widget and notification extensions on the same device). Deleting the app
deletes this data. There is no account, no cloud sync, no backup performed
by us. Your data may be included in your own device backups, which you
control.

## What we collect

Nothing.

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising identifiers, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- No account, email, or name.
- No network transmission of usage data — the app's screen-time features
  work entirely offline.

## Purchases

Wasted Pro is sold through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Your payment is
processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your
purchase entitlement locally through Apple's frameworks.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a technical log **on your device** to help diagnose problems.
It is never uploaded. If you choose to export it and send it to us, you
will see its full contents first, and sharing it is entirely your decision.

## Children

Wasted is a self-monitoring tool for your own device. It uses Apple's
*individual* Screen Time authorization, not parental controls, and is not
directed at children.

## Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP and similar)

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal
data an organisation holds about you. We hold none. Every record the app
creates is on your device, under your control: you can view it in the app
and destroy it by deleting the app. Since no personal data is collected,
sold, or shared, there is nothing further to request from us.

## Changes

If a future version of Wasted ever changes any of the above — for example,
by adding optional cloud sync — this policy will be updated first, the
change will be opt-in, and the app will say so plainly before anything
leaves your device.
